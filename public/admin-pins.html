Manage PINs

Each person signs in with their Recykal Google account and then enters a PIN. Set the PIN here and pass it to them yourself — over a call or in person, not over email. Nobody can change their own PIN.

PINs are 6–12 digits and are stored only as a scrypt hash — nobody, including you, can read back an existing PIN. If someone forgets theirs, set a new one. Five wrong attempts locks that account for 15 minutes; setting a new PIN clears the lock. Once every person below shows PIN set, remove the BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_PIN variable from Railway.
